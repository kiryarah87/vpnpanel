The Art of Simple Living

March 12, 2025 • 5 min read

In a world that constantly pushes us toward complexity, there is profound wisdom in simplicity. The Japanese concept of "ma" — the space between things — reminds us that what we leave out is just as important as what we include. Whether in design, relationships, or daily routines, simplicity creates room for what truly matters.
